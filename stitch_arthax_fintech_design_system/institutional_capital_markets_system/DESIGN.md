## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an institutional-grade, highly credible execution designed for precision capital markets, treasury management, and high-frequency equity trading. Pairing the authoritative gravitas of classical central banking with contemporary Material Design 3 surface architectures, it rejects decorative novelties—dispensing entirely with neon gradients, heavy card soup, and glassy distortions.

The emotional signature is calm, sober, and unyielding. It establishes total investor trust through:
- **Financial Architecture:** Rigorous spatial discipline where structural 1px hairline borders delineate high-density data.
- **Human Editorial Authority:** Editorial serif titling reminiscent of historical financial ledgers balanced against utilitarian sans and tabular numeric figures.
- **Restrained Materiality:** Tactile canvas planes and warm sand undertones that prevent visual fatigue during marathon market sessions.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by deep botanical minerals, parchment-toned structural grounds, and calibrated financial state indicators. 

### Core Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#173F35` - Deep Forest):** Applied to top-level navigation masts, definitive CTA elements, active state fills, and authoritative brand anchor points.
- **Secondary (`#2F7468` - Market Teal):** Serves interactive secondary structures, focused tab underlines, categorical tags, and segmented filter states.
- **Tertiary (`#DCEEE8` - Pale Mint):** Provides soft, non-disruptive tinted fills for informational highlights, active list row selections, and badge substrates.
- **Neutral Dark (`#242622` - Charcoal):** Primary text and data display ink. Replaces harsh pure black to reduce eye strain while retaining strict AAA contrast against all light containers.
- **Surface Canvas (`#F8F9F6`):** The global viewport background, offering a balanced off-white that absorbs screen glare.
- **Warm Sand Surface (`#F4F0E8`):** Applied selectively to contextual workspaces, secondary sidebars, and order placement docks to denote tactile, actionable zones.

### Market Semantics & Currency Accents
- **Positive / Gain (`#287A55`):** Denotes upward basis movement, ask fills, and positive yields. Never saturated into neon; calibrated for legibility on white and pale mint containers.
- **Negative / Loss (`#B94A43`):** Denotes bids retracted, downside volatility, and deficit metrics.
- **Currency Accent (`#A8742A` - Arth Gold):** Reserved strictly for native balance figures, ledger settlement units, and premium exchange tiers. It is never used for general interactive alerts or decorative borders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy divides cognitive responsibilities:
- **IBM Plex Serif** brings architectural structure and editorial provenance to primary market views, macro indices, portal mastheads, and high-level portfolio summaries.
- **Inter** provides high-legibility clarity for operational controls, data table labels, dense transactional listings, and analytical form fields.
- **Tabular Numerics Rule:** All numbers, volumes, prices, tick streams, and ledger timestamps must enforce font-feature-settings `tnum` (tabular figures) and `zero` (slashed zero) to prevent layout shifting during real-time WebSocket data updates.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts conform to a continuous 8-point base grid (with a strict 4-point subgrid for dense order book rows, mini-charts, and input padding).

### Viewport Structures
- **Desktop (1280px+):** Asymmetric multi-pane institutional layout featuring a top compact market ticker strip (32px fixed height), a 12-column analytical workspace with 16px gutters, and a persistent 360px sticky execution dock on the right rail.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Reflows to an 8-column layout. The order execution dock collapses into a sliding bottom-sheet drawer triggered by fixed bottom action controls.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with 8px margins. High-density data tables enable horizontal touch-swiping with the instrument symbol column pinned to the left edge.

## Elevation & Depth

In alignment with institutional safety and crisp readability, this system abandons blur-heavy diffusion in favor of **Tonal Layering and Hairline Boundaries**.

- **Hairline Dividers:** Structural separation relies on a continuous `1px solid rgba(23, 63, 53, 0.12)` border. Panels do not float disconnected; they share clean architectural boundaries.
- **Surface Elevation Levels:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#F8F9F6` baseline background.
  - **Level 1 (Panels & Sheets):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` bounded by hairline dividers. Used for order books, active charts, and analytical tables.
  - **Level 2 (Active Docks & Context Panels):** Warm Sand tint `#F4F0E8` paired with a 1px border. Identifies actionable zones such as trade placement tickets.
  - **Level 3 (Modals & Menus):** Pure white elevated solely with a crisp ambient shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(23, 63, 53, 0.08), 0 1px 2px rgba(23, 63, 53, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The shape geometry uses subtle softening (Level 1 - Soft) that retains a disciplined, technical profile:
- **Base Components:** Buttons, inputs, segmented bars, and table cells carry a sharp `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- **Containment Surfaces:** Data cards, slide-over panels, and dialog sheets use `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Micro-Indicators:** Status pills, trading badges, and execution flags use `0.125rem` (2px) to maximize screen efficiency and preserve spatial alignment with numerical columns.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Filled Primary:** Background `#173F35`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `0.25rem`. High-emphasis actions (e.g., "Place Buy Order"). Hover state darkens slightly to `#102B24` with no translation jump.
- **Execution Buy / Sell:** Buy buttons utilize `#287A55` fill; Sell buttons utilize `#B94A43` fill. Both enforce white tabular typography for rapid transaction identification.
- **Outlined / Secondary:** Surface transparent, border `1px solid #173F35`, text `#173F35`. For secondary analytical switches or export commands.

### Data Tables & Order Books
- Row height is constrained to a compact `32px` or comfortable `40px`.
- Every cell uses `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1`.
- Row selection transitions into `#DCEEE8` (Pale Mint) at 40% opacity.
- Depth ladders visualize volume via an anchor-left horizontal bar fill using `rgba(40, 122, 85, 0.1)` for bids and `rgba(185, 74, 67, 0.1)` for asks.

### Input Fields
- Enclosed containers with a solid white background, `1px solid rgba(23, 63, 53, 0.2)` border, and `0.25rem` radius.
- In focus state, the border shifts to `#173F35` with a crisp `1px` inner ring.
- Suffix accessories (e.g., "ARTH", "USD", "LIMIT") render in `Inter` semi-bold at 75% opacity to avoid visual clutter.

### Horizontal Ticker Strips
- Compact continuous banner across the sub-masthead (`height: 32px`).
- Background `#F4F0E8` with a bottom hairline border `1px solid rgba(23, 63, 53, 0.12)`.
- Symbol codes render in bold uppercase `Inter`, paired with real-time numeric deltas rendered in green (`#287A55`) or red (`#B94A43`).

### Sticky Order Dock
- Persistent workspace on `#F4F0E8` Warm Sand canvas, separated from the chart matrix by a `1px solid rgba(23, 63, 53, 0.15)` border.
- Organizes side-by-side transaction tabs ("Limit", "Market", "Stop") with immediate visual fee breakdowns, slippage alerts, and estimated settlement totals rendered in `#A8742A` (Arth Gold).
