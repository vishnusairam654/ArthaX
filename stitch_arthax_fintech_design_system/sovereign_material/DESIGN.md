# Material 3 Design Guidelines for ARTHAX

All components must strictly adhere to Google Material Design 3 (M3) specifications:
- **Surface System:** Use distinct M3 surface roles (`surface`, `surface-container-lowest`, `surface-container-low`, `surface-container`, `surface-container-high`, `surface-container-highest`) to denote visual depth instead of heavy shadows or artificial borders.
- **Color Roles & Tonal Palette:** Always pair container colors with their respective `on-*` tokens (e.g., `primary-container` with `on-primary-container`, `secondary-container` with `on-secondary-container`, `surface-variant` with `on-surface-variant`).
- **M3 Shape Tokens:** 
  - Cards & Container Panels: `rounded-2xl` (16px) or `rounded-3xl` (28px) for containers.
  - Buttons: Standard Filled, Tonal, Outlined, and Elevated buttons with `rounded-full` (9999px).
  - Chips & Filter Pills: `rounded-lg` (8px) with `border border-outline-variant`.
  - Text Fields & Inputs: M3 Filled or Outlined text field styles with active bottom indicators or 1px outline with floating labels.
- **State Layers & Interactions:** Implement 8% / 12% hover and pressed opacity overlays for buttons, cards, and list items.
- **Typography:** Display & Title using Newsreader/Geist Serif with clean editorial dignity; technical and interactive labels in clean geometric sans with tabular numerals for all ARTH balances.
