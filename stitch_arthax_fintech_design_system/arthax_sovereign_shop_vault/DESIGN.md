## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the rare intersection of sovereign classical finance and playful gamified acquisition. Built for high-tier asset collection, institutional staking, and vault custody, the aesthetic channels the gravity of historic national mints, private bank reserves, and royal charters—counterbalanced by the tactile delight of collectible prestige systems.

The emotional arc is deliberate: users feel the uncompromising security and permanence of an ancient central depository alongside the thrill of uncovering museum-grade rarity assets. 

The aesthetic marries **Neoclassical Institutionalism** with **Modern Tactile Materiality**:
- **Parchment and Sovereign Indigo:** The visual tone moves away from sterile corporate greys, grounding itself in warm archival parchment substrates paired with deep royal blue and velvet indigo ink tones.
- **Radiant Metallics & Rarity Dialectics:** Key interactive affordances, badges, and asset frames carry the warmth of refined gold bullion, accented by a strictly codified four-tier chromatic rarity model (Normal, Rare, Epic, Gold).
- **Tactile Prestige:** Interactive surfaces preserve physical weight through delicate hairline borders, crisp editorial typography, and high-fidelity tonal elevation.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture relies on deep, authoritative blues grounded on luminous parchment surfaces, highlighted with antique golds and codified tier accents.

### Foundation & Inks
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF8F5`):** Warm sovereign parchment, reducing screen glare while projecting archival heritage.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` to `#F4EDE0`):** Pure white container surfaces layered with muted cream substrates for inset vaults and ledger trays.
- **Primary Sovereign Blue (`#1E3A5F`):** Anchoring brand authority, used for master interactive elements, primary headers, and state boundaries.
- **Institutional Indigo (`#2A3B6A`):** Supporting tier for structural borders, hover depths, and deep navigational backdrops.
- **Sovereign Ink (`#0F1E31`):** Deepest navy-black text color used for headlines and high-emphasis balances.

### Radiant Accents
- **Arth Gold Primary (`#A8742A`):** Burnished gold for transaction actions, premium locks, and master vault seals.
- **Arth Gold Luminous (`#C59A45`):** Specular highlight gold for borders, active glows, and state toggles.
- **Gold Cream (`#FDF8F0` / `#F4EDE0`):** Soft foundational fill for highlighted ledger items, banners, and premium chip containers.

### Rarity Classification Tokens
- **Normal (`#64748B`):** Archival Slate for foundational standard-grade assets and common ledger entries.
- **Rare (`#2563EB`):** Sapphire Azure for augmented assets, elevated yields, and verified vaults.
- **Epic (`#7C3AED`):** Imperial Amethyst for enchanted, high-tier sovereign relics and compounding multipliers.
- **Gold (`#D97706` / `#A8742A`):** Sovereign Relic Gold denoting maximum prestige, non-fungible charters, and zero-tier items.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs classical numismatic authority with modern financial clarity.

- **Editorial Authority (`Newsreader`):** Applied to primary headings, modal vault titles, prestige tier badges, and high-order marketplace banners. It evokes historical treaty texts, classical currency engravings, and private banking ledgers.
- **Structural Utility (`Inter`):** Drives all narrative body text, metadata fields, controls, tooltips, and micro-labels. Its neutral, engineered proportions balance Newsreader's expressive classical serifs.
- **Tabular Engine (`Inter` with `font-feature-settings: "tnum", "cv05"`):** All balance counters, yield rates, modifier percentages, and transaction ledger rows must enforce tabular fixed-width numerals to eliminate visual jitter during real-time balance calculations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an uncompromising 8pt baseline rhythm, paired with a sovereign 12-column dynamic grid designed to handle both data-dense inventory matrices and ceremonial item showcases.

### Grid Rules
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, dynamic margins maxing at 1360px container width. In vault inspect mode, the layout splits into an asymmetric 7:5 master-detail ratio.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 16px gutters, 32px safe margins. Marketplace listings reflow to 2- or 3-column configurations.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px margins. Navigational systems collapse into pinned bottom tab structures with quick-access vault drawers.

### Spacing Cadence
- Inset cards use `space-base` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px) internal cushioning.
- Data tables adhere to a tight vertical density (`space-sm` / 8px vertical padding per row) to ensure institutional scanning efficiency.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages crisp structural borders, layered warm parchment surfaces, and low-opacity sovereign blue ambient shadows to replicate the presence of architectural safe boxes and physical minted bullion.

### Layer Hierarchy
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** `#FAF8F5` flat sovereign parchment.
- **Layer 1 (Recessed/Inset):** `#F4EDE0` with an inner hairline border (`rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.08)`) used for empty item sockets, locked vaults, and balance counters.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated Card):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-delicate outline (`rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.08)`) and ambient diffusion `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.05)`.
- **Layer 3 (Active / Floating / Popover):** `#FFFFFF` anchored by dual shadows: a sharp 1px contact edge `0 1px 2px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.08)` and an expanded aura `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.12)`.
- **Layer 4 (Modal & Sovereign Focus):** Floating parchment surfaced with a radiant outer rim `0 0 0 1px rgba(168, 116, 42, 0.25)` and volumetric diffusion `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 30, 49, 0.25)`.

### Shimmer & Specular Lighting
Gold-tier assets, equipped modifiers, and affirmative sovereign action buttons carry a delicate linear gradient sheen (`135deg, #FAF8F5 0%, #F4EDE0 100%`) complemented by a 1px top-edge highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).

## Shapes

The shape system employs strict structural discipline (`roundedness: 2`, where base components use `0.5rem` / 8px). This matches the chiseled, disciplined profile of legal documents, metal ingots, and ledger books while softening corner points for interactive touchscreens.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base (8px / `0.5rem`):** Marketplace asset cards, input fields, dropdown menus, and data summary modules.
- **Large (16px / `1rem`):** Primary modal displays, vault detail sheets, and hero banners.
- **Pill / Capsule (9999px):** Category tabs, rarity chips, active item modifiers, and tactile action triggers.
- **Hairlines:** Interactive borders strictly measure 1px, reinforcing precision craftsmanship.

- Inset asset showcase area `#F4EDE0` featuring an embossed archival frame.
- Card footer displays item name in Newsreader 18px semibold, with tabular price/modifier aligned right in Inter 14px numeric.
- Hover produces an elevation shift to Layer 3 accompanied by an Arth Gold hairline rim transition.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Recessed `#FAF8F5` surface with an 8px border radius and 1px inset border `rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.15)`.
- Focus state reveals a dual ring: 1px sovereign blue `#1E3A5F` and 3px outer halo `rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.08)`.
- Monospaced / tabular numerals for all asset transfer quantity fields.

### Navigation Pill System
- Segmented filter controls enclosed in an architectural pill track (`#F4EDE0`).
- Active item snaps onto a pure white elevated pill with a 1px `#1E3A5F` (10% opacity) border and micro shadow.

### Checkboxes & Radios

- Unchecked: `#FFFFFF` with 1.5px `#1E3A5F` border (30% opacity).
- Checked: Sovereign blue `#1E3A5F` fill with crisp white institutional checkmark or central gold disc (`#C59A45`).
