## Brand & Style

The design system projects sovereign stability, architectural poise, and institutional authority. Designed for a premier Tier-1 digital financial ecosystem and public central ledger guide, it balances the timeless gravitas of central banking literature with the razor-sharp clarity of modern high-performance interfaces.

### Core Philosophy
- **Institutional Weight over Ephemera:** Avoid trend-driven neon glows, synthetic gradient blobs, and floating card soup. Surfaces are warm, physical, and grounded, echoing archival ledger paper and polished stone.
- **Architectural Asymmetry:** Reject generic multi-column dashboard templates. Layouts employ deliberate editorial pacing—pairing expansive typographic statements with dense, data-rich ledger modules and precision micro-diagrams.
- **Micro-Precision & Restraint:** Visual distinction is communicated through hairline registration rules, calibrated typographic hierarchy, and surgical placements of reserved metallic accents.

## Colors

The palette operates in high-clarity daylight, anchored by warm, paper-inspired foundational tones, balanced against deep institutional blues, royal indigo accents, and a singular reserved gold leaf tone.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Sovereign Blue (`#1E3A5F`):** The institutional bedrock. Used for authoritative titles, high-priority interactive states, brand marks, and core structural dividers.
- **Secondary Royal Indigo (`#2A3B6A`):** Used for analytical groupings, subtle contextual navigation, secondary actions, and tonal structural backdrops.
- **Tertiary Arth Gold (`#A8742A`):** Applied with strict restraint (never exceeding 5% of viewport coverage). Reserved for audited verification seals, sovereign status badges, active timeline indices, and focused fiscal indicators.
- **Base Surfaces:**
  - Archival Canvas (`#FBFBF9`): Default screen background; reduces eye fatigue while referencing tactile bond paper.
  - Recessed Stone (`#F2EFE7`): Subordinate surfaces, data wells, timeline tracks, and table headers.
  - Elevated Pure Surface (`#FFFFFF`): High-clarity focal modules, modals, and verified transaction receipts.
- **Hairline Registration (`rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.08)`):** Replaces heavy border strokes with 1px architectural rules, ensuring crisp compartmentalization without visual bulk.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial prestige of `Newsreader` with the neutral, hyper-legible execution of `Inter`.

### Principles
- **Editorial Headlines:** Display and Headline roles utilize `Newsreader` with natural optical balance, providing an authoritative institutional voice. Display styles adopt relaxed, deliberate line heights reminiscent of fiscal prospectuses.
- **Hyper-Systematic Engine:** Body and interactive metadata rely exclusively on `Inter`. All data-driven tables, asset readouts, and timestamps must explicitly enable tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent alignment shifts during real-time valuation updates.
- **Archival Tags:** Small categorizers and micro-headers employ `label-caps` with uppercase styling and expanded tracking (+0.08em) for instantaneous indexing.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts reject uniform, repetitive grid cards in favor of dynamic asymmetric compositions: broad editorial lead-ins offset by dense tabular ledgers and technical timelines.

### Spatial Discipline
- **Scale:** Built on a rigorous 4px baseline (`0.25rem` step). Content gutters sit at `1rem` on compact displays, widening to `2rem` across wide viewports.
- **Asymmetric Splits:** Desktop viewports employ 12-column architectural rhythms. Rather than balanced 6+6 divisions, prefer 5+7 or 4+8 layouts—allowing one side to act as an expansive editorial premise while the other houses transactional rails.
- **Vertical Air:** Section transitions employ generous breathing room (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) juxtaposed against tight, dense micro-spacings (`space-2xs` to `space-xs`) within complex fiscal ledgers.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, floating dropshadows, blurred backdrops, and artificial depth. Visual hierarchy is established strictly via tonal surface layering and crisp registration hairlines.

### Stratification
1. **Base Tier (Recessed / Canvas):** `#FBFBF9` and `#F2EFE7` represent un-elevated physical terrain. Used for canvas backgrounds and ledger audit troughs.
2. **Structural Tier (Plates):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces layered with 1px perimeter borders in `rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.08)`. No shadow is cast; separation is achieved entirely through chromatic contrast against the warmer canvas.
3. **Transient Tier (Overlays & Menus):** High-priority popovers and command palettes receive a singular, ultra-subtle architectural shadow:
   `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.06), 0 0 0 1px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.1)`.
   The shadow is tinted in institutional blue rather than generic black, maintaining tonal cohesion.

## Shapes

Shapes reflect precision-machined geometry. Roundedness is intentionally constrained to level `1` (Soft: `0.25rem` / `4px`), ensuring an architectural, disciplined silhouette.

### Curvature Rules
- **Interactive Triggers & Inputs:** Bound by exact `4px` radii (`0.25rem`). This prevents consumer-app bubble aesthetics and enforces institutional authority.
- **Structural Modules & Ledger Sheets:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) outer corners maximum.
- **Status Pills & Micro-Flags:** Explicitly exempt from pill caps. Status tags must utilize crisp `2px` corners or exact `0px` chamfered brackets to match ledger typography.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary:** Filled `#1E3A5F` with crisp `#FFFFFF` typography. 4px roundedness, `space-sm` vertical by `space-lg` horizontal padding. Hover shifts smoothly to `#2A3B6A` without elevation change.
- **Secondary (Hairline):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by a 1px `rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.16)` perimeter. Text rendered in `#1E3A5F`. Hover initiates a warm stone wash (`#F2EFE7`).
- **Tertiary (Sovereign Gold Accent):** Used strictly for certified operations or primary ledger signing: `#A8742A` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- High-contrast `#FFFFFF` fields framed with 1px `rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.16)` borders.
- Labels sit above in `label-caps` styling (`#5A6472`).
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#1E3A5F` with a concurrent 1px outer ring in `rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.12)`. No high-saturation glows.

### Ledger Cards & Modules
- Avoids standalone floating cards. Modules are constructed as unified ledger plates featuring continuous 1px rules dividing header, content, and audit footer.
- Header bars utilize subtle stone fills (`#F2EFE7`) with Newsreader headlines, while body matrices display values in tabular Inter.

### Verification Badges & Chips
- Crisp, non-rounded rect tags with 2px corners.
- Verified status: `#F2EFE7` fill, 1px border in `rgba(168, 116, 42, 0.3)`, text in `#A8742A` with `label-caps` tracking.

### Timelines & Micro-Diagrams
- Vertical linear tracks defined by a continuous 1px rule (`rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.12)`).
- Node markers are sharp 8x8px diamond or square glyphs filled with `#1E3A5F` or `#A8742A`, anchoring audit events directly to the timeline track.
