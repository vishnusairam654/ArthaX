```text
  Theme: Forest Green

BANK PORTAL
→ Operate one commercial bank (Nava, Samaya, Setu, Sthira, Vayu: Customers, Accounts, Loans, Local Operations)
  Theme: Indigo

  Theme: Sovereign Light Gold (Imperial Champagne Canvas, Gilded Gold Borders, Deep Bronze Authority Typography)
```
